📣 JOIN THE ASIS BOARD FOR 2025–2026!

Do you have passion for community, culture, and making meaningful connections with other Indonesian Trojans at USC? We’re excited to open applications for the 2025–2026 ASIS Board!

We are ASIS (Association of Indonesian Students at USC), a student-led organization dedicated to fostering a warm, supportive space for Indonesians on campus. From our signature events like HOPA, Makan Bareng, and Fall/Spring Welcoming, to casual hangouts and collaborative initiatives with other orgs, ASIS is all about creating a home away from home.

We’re looking for enthusiastic, responsible, and creative individuals to join our team and help shape the ASIS experience next year for the Indonesian community at USC. This is your chance to contribute, grow your leadership skills, and build lasting friendships.

Does this sound like you?
Fill in this application so that we can get to know you better and potentially welcome you to our team!

🗓 Deadline: August 20, 2025 at 11:59 PM PST
